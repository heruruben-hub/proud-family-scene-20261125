An entry for the make.fun bounty "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene":
write one short family scene, warm, funny and heartfelt, in no more than
280 characters.

## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly this text to `/output/entry.txt` (222 characters,
no trailing newline):

```text
Dad finally fixed the wobbly table leg after three years, and Mum clapped like he had won the World Cup. The kids chanted his name, the dog barked along, and Dad stood there grinning, holding the screwdriver like a trophy.
```

## Why this scene

It is a whole small household in one breath. The warmth is real and
unforced: a family that turns a three-year-old wobbly table leg into a
celebration, because that is what families do with small victories. The
funny turn lands at the end — Dad holding the screwdriver like a trophy —
a wry image any family recognises, and the detail that makes the scene
specific instead of a greeting card: the wobbly leg, the three years, the
dog barking along with the chant. And it answers the brief on its own
terms: the proudest moment in the scene is a father being cheered by his
own family, the kind of pride a proud judge smiles at.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
